# Architecture

Foreseer is a monorepo with four moving parts and one contract between
them: FORESEER-SPEC v0.1 plus its golden test vectors.

```
players / operators
        |
        v
  server/        Nest.js + SQLite: epochs, receipts, verify API, billing
        |
        v
  tee/           Go FCE extension on Flare Confidential Compute (signs everything)
        |
        v
  contracts      ForeseerInstructionSender + OperatorBond on Flare
        ^
        |
  packages/ts    foreseer.ts: the reference engine and the player's verifier
```

## The spec and the vectors are the real interface

`spec/SPEC.md` defines every byte: the HMAC stream, rejection sampling, rule
canonicalization, EIP-712 structures, commit/reveal, and the Merkle tree.
`spec/vectors/` holds machine-checkable JSON derived from fixed seeds and
keys: derivation blocks, draw sequences (including forced rejection-path
cases), canonical rules with hashes, fully signed receipts, Merkle trees
with proofs, and one complete epoch.

Three implementations must reproduce those vectors byte for byte, and do:

- **TypeScript** (`packages/ts`): the normative reference, plus the browser
  verifier bundle.
- **Go** (`tee/go/internal/engine`): the enclave engine; its test suite
  replays every vector including every RFC6979 signature.
- **Solidity** (`tee/contracts`): the anchoring contract accepts the golden
  epoch close signature onchain via ecrecover.

If you port Foreseer to a fourth language, the vectors are your conformance
suite and three independent referees already exist.

## Epoch lifecycle

1. `openEpoch`: the TEE creates a seed, the commitment goes out (and, once
   deployed, onchain through `sendOpenEpoch`, which also splits the paid
   epoch fee between treasury and operator pool).
2. `play`: bets stream in; each returns a signed receipt immediately. State
   (nonces per clientSeed, sequential betIds) lives in SQLite server-side,
   so a restart mid-epoch loses nothing.
3. `closeEpoch`: the TEE reveals the seed, builds the Merkle tree, and signs
   an `EpochClose` structure binding commitment, seed, root, and count.
4. `anchorEpoch`: anyone can submit that signed close to the contract; it
   verifies the signature against the attested TEE identity and stores the
   root. From here every receipt is provable forever.

## Trust boundaries

- The **server** is untrusted for fairness. It orchestrates and stores, but
  every artifact it hands out is TEE-signed and player-recomputable.
- The **TEE** is trusted only as far as Flare attestation: the registry
  binds an identity address to a measured image, and that address is what
  every signature check expects.
- The **contract** is trustless verification: it does not believe anyone;
  it checks the EIP-712 signature, the low-s rule, and the SHA-256
  commitment binding before anchoring anything.
- The **player** trusts open source math running on their own machine.

## Where things live

| Path | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
