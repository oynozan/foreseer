# What is Foreseer

Foreseer is a provably fair engine for iGaming. It answers one question with
mathematics instead of trust: **how do I know the house did not cheat me?**

Game outcomes are computed inside a Trusted Execution Environment (a TEE, on
Flare Confidential Compute), committed to before you bet, and fully
verifiable by you afterwards, in your own browser, with open source code.

## The one minute version

1. Before any bet, the TEE generates a secret 32 byte `serverSeed` and
   publishes its SHA-256 hash onchain. This is the **commitment**: the house
   locks in its randomness before knowing your bets.
2. You bet with your own `clientSeed`. Your outcome is a pure function of
   the server seed, your seed, and a counter. The house cannot pick outcomes
   without breaking its commitment; you cannot predict them without the
   secret seed.
3. Every outcome comes back instantly as a **receipt** signed by the TEE's
   identity key. Think of it as a cryptographic betting slip.
4. When the epoch closes, the seed is revealed and a Merkle root of all
   receipts is anchored onchain. Now everyone can recompute everything.

## A real receipt

This is an actual receipt from the golden test vectors: a dice bet needing
over 5000, which rolled 3725 and lost.

```json
{
    "receipt": {
        "specVersion": 1,
        "codeVersion": "0x6094010faf9dafee4b20d2dd6d5bc2ffcbb480ee3d8f3226c5625d5076f7a28b",
        "epochId": 1,
        "betId": 0,
        "seedCommit": "0x630dcd2966c4336691125448bbb25b4ff412a49c732db2c8abc1b8581bd710dd",
        "clientSeed": "alice",
        "nonce": 0,
        "ruleHash": "0x7939b82e6b02921475a471681eeacef7b1f41b2457e8e4898f65c113e7016a87",
        "draws": [3725],
        "win": false,
        "payoutBp": 0,
        "timestamp": 1755000000
    },
    "signature": "0x040650963bbdc90b8ab031ca1c3c029c1fa7ae60138fbf06b65631a2155370aa0d92f1961f67878ed41e469d20306092c95587ff7045d26c032ae3915f6dae6c1c"
}
```

Every field is covered by the signature. If the house later claimed this bet
never happened, or that it rolled something else, the receipt proves
otherwise, and the Merkle anchor proves the receipt belongs to the epoch.

## Guarantees, stated honestly

Foreseer makes outcomes **tamper evident**, not magically fair. The TEE
necessarily knows outcomes during an epoch; what protects you is that its
code is attested by Flare, its seed was committed before your bet, and every
receipt is recomputable by anyone after the reveal. Three independent
implementations (TypeScript, Go, Solidity) reproduce the protocol byte for
byte against shared golden vectors, so no single codebase is trusted either.
