Foreseer is a provably fair engine for iGaming, built on Flare Confidential Compute TEEs. Outcomes are computed inside attested hardware, committed before you bet, and verifiable by you afterwards.

Open the receipt verifier

How an epoch works

  1. The TEE generates a secret 32 byte serverSeed and publishes SHA256(serverSeed) onchain before any bet is placed.
  2. Each bet is resolved as HMAC-SHA256(serverSeed, clientSeed:nonce:block) with unbiased rejection sampling. You choose the clientSeed.
  3. Every outcome comes back as an EIP-712 receipt signed by the TEE identity key.
  4. At epoch close the seed is revealed and a Merkle root over all receipts is anchored onchain through ForeseerInstructionSender.

Specification and code

The protocol is normatively specified in FORESEER-SPEC v0.1. Three independent implementations reproduce it byte for byte against shared golden vectors: the TypeScript library @foreseer/sdk, the Go TEE extension, and the Solidity anchoring contract.

Verify your own bet

Ask your operator for the epoch id and bet id, then use the verifier: it can fetch the receipt, rule, revealed seed, and Merkle proof from any Foreseer server, or you can paste them by hand. The library, and therefore the verdict, runs entirely on your machine.
